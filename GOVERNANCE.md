# Governance

DevTrellis Pulse is led by Frank Oduro, who is in charge of everything in the project.

- **Lead maintainer (Frank Oduro):** final authority over code, design, releases, security
  decisions, contributors and this document.
- **Minor contributors:** suggest and write changes through pull requests. The
  automation accounts and other contributors listed in [AUTHORS.md](AUTHORS.md) are in this group.
  Nothing from a minor contributor is merged or released without the lead maintainer.
- **Security sensitive changes:** anything touching commit policy, path permissions, network
  access or secret handling gets extra careful review by the lead maintainer.
- **Adding maintainers:** only by invitation from the lead maintainer.
- **Disagreements:** raise them with the lead maintainer, who decides.
- **Abandonment:** if the project goes unmaintained for 12 months, anyone may fork it under
  the Apache License 2.0.
